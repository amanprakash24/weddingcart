# Shaadi Shopping digital invitation templates

Master copies of the invitation templates. They are kept here so they are versioned and never lost. They are not served on the website; published invites live in `public/invitations/<slug>/`.

- `divine-union/`: the 3D Radha-Krishna / Shiv-Parvati scrolling invitation, first used for Sudhanshu & Shreya. See `divine-union/README.md` for how to create a client invite.
- `build-for-site.sh`: copies a finished invite into `public/invitations/<slug>/`.

To work on a client invite, copy `divine-union/` to `C:\Shaadi Shopping\Invitation Sample\templates\` (the working folder) and follow its README.
